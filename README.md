### 👨‍💻 About me

I'm an IT Engineer at an insurance brokerage in Salt Lake City, where I keep a hybrid Windows + cloud environment running and secure — endpoint health in Intune, vulnerability remediation, and the occasional 2 a.m. domain controller question. I came into security through a Criminology degree and an M.S. in Cybersecurity, which is a slightly unusual on-ramp, and I think it made me better at the part of this job that's really about people and access.

Lately I've been pulling hard toward **identity** — directory design, lifecycle automation, and the messy, interesting problem of who should be able to reach what. Most of what's here is me working that out in a lab and writing down what actually happened, including the parts that broke.

- 🔭 **Currently:** IT Engineer @ The Buckner Company — Intune, Entra ID, LAPS/BitLocker rollouts, phishing awareness program
- 🌱 **Learning:** Okta administration, Wazuh SIEM tuning, PowerShell automation for JML workflows
- 💬 **Ask me about:** Active Directory, Intune app packaging, hybrid identity, or how to break a lab environment three ways before lunch
